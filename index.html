Bindu R

Founder and Partner

Bindu R is the founder of BR Innovation & Technology and the visionary behind YANI, an adaptive AI companion designed to support children’s focus, emotions, and learning. With strong experience in business management, marketing, and innovation leadership, she is driving the company toward building accessible AI-powered learning systems for homes and schools across India. Under her leadership, BR Innovation & Technology developed the YANI prototype and successfully conducted early testing with children and schools, achieving strong engagement and positive educational impact.
