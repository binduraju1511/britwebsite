Quick answers for parents ♡

Yani is designed around parent-controlled settings, age-appropriate experiences, and a calm, ad-free environment.
Use the parent dashboard to review daily activity, set routines, and adjust how long different activities are available.
Yani is designed for children ages 3–13, with activities and guidance tailored to each stage of growth.
The parent experience is designed to keep data choices clear and manageable. You can review and control profile, voice, and activity settings.
Yani is being designed for multilingual families. Current language availability will always be shown clearly during setup.
Yani uses internet access for connected learning features and updates, while selected routines are designed to remain available offline.
