Our Story ☆✦

Built on purpose ♡

Yani is designed around three core pillars that shape everything we do.

Adaptive Learning

Yani adapts to each child’s pace and interests, making learning personal, engaging, and effective.

Emotional Intelligence

Yani listens, responds, and encourages—helping children express, understand, and manage their emotions.

Educational Robotics

A smart blend of AI and robotics that creates a safe, interactive, and fun learning companion.

Our vision for India ♡

We envision Yani becoming a trusted companion in homes, schools, and communities across India—making quality learning and emotional support accessible to every child, everywhere.
