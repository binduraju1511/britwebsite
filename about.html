About Us

Adaptive AI educational robotics designed to support children through intelligent learning and emotional engagement.

Our Journey of Innovation

Founded in 2025, BR Innovation & Technology is an AI educational robotics startup focused on developing and refining adaptive AI learning solutions designed to support children's focus, emotions, and education.

Our journey began with a vision to create an intelligent companion system that combines emotional understanding with personalized learning experiences. We have successfully developed our initial YANI prototype and conducted early-stage testing with children and educational environments, receiving encouraging engagement and positive feedback.

Based in Bangalore, our team is actively working on AI development, product enhancement, and educational innovation to build scalable solutions for homes, schools, and learning ecosystems across India.
